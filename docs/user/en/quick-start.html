Kids Galaxy quick start

Keep this page next to the projector.

The full steps are in the install guide and the daily guide.

Before the children arrive

About 5 minutes.

  • The computer is on and shows the desktop.
  • The projector is on and shows that computer.
  • Both tablets are on the same Wi-Fi as the computer.
  • The wall shows the dark sky.

Turn the room on

  • Turn on the computer. Wait for the desktop.
  • Turn on the projector. Choose HDMI.
  • On the computer, open `http://127.0.0.1:8000/`.
  • Press F11 so the picture fills the screen.
  • Wake the tablets.

A child draws

  • On the kid tablet, tap a shape and draw.
  • Tap Launch.
  • Look at the wall. The new planet should appear.

A grown-up

  • Open Kids Galaxy Manager. It is not the drawing app.
  • Tap EN / FR to change that tablet.
  • Tap Projector language to change the wall words.
  • Tap Print for paper. Tap STL for a 3D file.

Turn the room off

  • Shut down the computer from the power menu.
  • Turn off the projector.
  • Let the tablets sleep.

If the wall is dark

  • Check both power lights.
  • Check the HDMI cable and the HDMI source.
  • On the computer, open `http://127.0.0.1:8000/`.
